Sustainable Tourism & Travelife

Consult an Expert
  • Travelife certification support: gap assessment, documentation, and audit preparation from first checklist to certificate.
  • Operational sustainability audits: energy, water, waste, and procurement reviews tailored to camp and lodge operations.
  • Community impact tracking: measurable frameworks for the local employment, sourcing, and conservation programmes your guests ask about.

PILLAR 02

Off-the-Grid Building & Infrastructure

Building where the grid doesn't reach, for remote lodges, humanitarian camps, and eco-conscious developers.

Request a Baseline Audit
  • IFC EDGE certification: software modelling for energy, water, and material efficiency, led by accredited EDGE Experts.
  • Sustainable design consultancy: passive cooling, renewable integration, and circular waste designed in from day one.
  • Implementation hub: a direct line to vetted partners for materials, energy, water, and roofing, so specification becomes procurement.
  • Remote-site feasibility: baseline audits that tell you what a self-sustaining site will actually take, before you commit capital.

Why we exist

Mission & vision.

Mission

To catalyse the transition of Africa’s commercial, humanitarian, and tourism sectors towards regenerative, resource-efficient, and internationally certified practices. Through expert multidisciplinary consultancy, strategic global partnerships, and the targeted deployment of cutting-edge green technologies, we deliver measurable, full-cycle environmental solutions that drive operational excellence, ensure regulatory compliance, and foster deep ecological harmony.

Vision

To stand as the premier architect of sustainable transformation across Africa, forging a resilient future where the built environment, humanitarian operations, and ecotourism seamlessly coexist with and actively regenerate the natural ecosystem.
