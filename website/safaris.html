The hidden Kenya

A safari your children will still talk about in twenty years.

Anyone can show you a lion. We show you what it takes to keep the lion there: a camp that turns kitchen waste into cooking gas, a borehole that waters a village and a herd, a classroom where rangers train.

Our tours are high-end and eco-conscious, built with the same partners and lodges we certify, so the sustainability you see is real, measured, and working.

🔥 Waste-to-energy in action

Watch a bio-digester turn breakfast scraps into the flame that cooks dinner.

💧 The water walk

Follow a drop of water through a closed circular system, from source to treatment and back to the land.

🌿 Conservation classrooms

Meet the rangers, trackers, and community teams behind the conservancies.
