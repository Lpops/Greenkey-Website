# Website Generation Prompt — Greenkey Africa

Copy everything below the line into your website builder or AI tool.

---

Build a visually stunning, modern 3-page website for **Greenkey Africa**, an integrated sustainability consultancy and eco-tourism provider in East Africa. The site must feel dynamic, fresh, clean, and modern, with very simple and intuitive navigation. Tagline: **"Sustainability from strategy to experience."**

## Brand & Tone
- Positioning: from technical auditor to multi-pillar sustainability & impact agency. "We don't just certify buildings — we help design and run self-sustaining infrastructure in Africa's most remote environments, and take you to see it in action."
- Audience: dual — high-level corporate/NGO/lodge clients (professional, credible) AND families seeking educational travel (warm, emotive).
- Visual mood: "impact and environment," not "blueprints and hardhats."

## Design System
**Typography**
- Headers, display text, and ALL button text: **Syne Bold (700/800)** — geometric, characterful, tight letter-spacing (-0.02em on large headings).
- Body text: a clean neutral grotesque (e.g. Instrument Sans), 17px base, relaxed line-height.

**Color palette**
- Page background: warm cream/ecru `#F4F0E7`; alternating section bands in soft taupe `#E9E2D3`; cards on slightly darker cream `#EDE7D9`.
- Ink (headings/text): deep teal-navy `#14333C`; secondary text `#45606A`.
- Primary accent: vivid leaf green `#72AC28` (hover `#5C8F1B`) — used for pill buttons, links, eyebrow labels.
- Safari/warm accent (B2C sections only): golden amber `#D89A3E` and terracotta `#C2622E` with warm gradient panels.
- Dark panels/footer: the deep teal `#14333C` with cream text and a soft green radial glow.

**Components & styling**
- Fully rounded pill buttons (border-radius 100px), green primary, ghost/outline secondary, gold pills on safari content.
- Generous rounded corners on cards and imagery (16–24px), soft deep shadows on hover with a subtle lift (translateY).
- Eyebrow labels: small uppercase, letter-spaced, with a short accent dash before the text.
- Subtle paper-grain noise texture over the background; lots of whitespace; max content width ~1200px.

**Motion**
- Staggered fade-up entrance on hero content; slow zoom-out on hero imagery.
- Scroll-triggered reveals (fade + rise) on all sections; gentle image scale on card hover.
- Sticky nav that turns to frosted cream glass with blur on scroll. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Structure (max 3 pages)

**1. Home**
- Full-viewport photographic hero, white Syne headline "Sustainability from strategy to experience", two CTAs: "Consult an Expert" (green pill) and "Start Your Journey" (glass ghost pill).
- "Three pillars, one integrated practice" — three photo cards: Sustainable Tourism & Travelife / Off-the-Grid Building & Infrastructure / Humanitarian & Corporate ESG.
- "Building where the grid doesn't reach" split section (copy + photo), followed by three Problem → Solution → Partner bridge cards (Organic Waste → Biogas digesters → Sistema.bio; Building Materials → Sustainable surfaces → Tactile Kenya; Water Systems → Circular water → Bio Liff Kenya).
- Warm gradient safari teaser panel: "The backstage of conservation."
- Advisors teaser strip ("The brain trust behind Greenkey") linking to the experts section.
- Client logo wall (grayscale, color on hover): JCG, Landmark, PDM, Atua Enkop Africa, IPS, City Lodge Hotels, GREA, I&M Bank, Betts Townsend Taylor, ADvTECH, Esteel.
- Horizontal-scroll project gallery; dark "Consult an expert" CTA panel; footer with IFC EDGE Expert + Travelife Partner badges.

**2. Consultancy Services**
- Three deep pillar sections with sticky left headers and checklist-style deliverables (Travelife certification, IFC EDGE modelling, ESG reporting, etc.). CTA on the building pillar: "Request a Baseline Audit."
- Implementation Partners hub — featurette rows for Tactile Kenya (materials), Sistema.bio (energy), Climatile & Blutech (infrastructure), Bio Liff Kenya (water), Ibis Tours & Travel (logistics).
- "Our People & Advisors" grid: filter chips (All / Tourism Experts / Green Building Specialists / Humanitarian Advisors), cards with headshot, name, title, 2-sentence value prop; clicking opens a bio modal with LinkedIn link. Use placeholder profiles until final bios are supplied.
- Consultation enquiry form.

**3. Safaris & Tours (distinctly warmer)**
- Golden-gradient hero: "See the backstage of conservation." CTA: "Start Your Journey" (gold pill).
- "The Hidden Kenya — beyond the Big Five" narrative with experience cards (bio-digester demo, water walk, conservation classrooms).
- Three sample itinerary cards (4 / 6 / 8 days) with day counts and highlight lists.
- Trust panel: "We certify the places we take you."
- Emotive family inquiry form: adults, children & ages, travel dates, curiosity checkboxes (wildlife, green tech, water, community, rangers, photography).

## Notes
- Logo not final — use a clean placeholder mark in nav and footer, easy to swap.
- Remove all ISO certification references; feature IFC EDGE and Travelife prominently (footer badges + building page).
- Mobile-first responsive; accessible contrast; semantic HTML.
